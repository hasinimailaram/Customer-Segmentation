Project Overview

This project focuses on customer segmentation using Power BI. Customers are grouped into different segments based on their Annual Income and Spending Score to understand purchasing behavior and generate targeted business insights.

Objectives
Analyze customer spending patterns.
Segment customers based on income and spending behavior.
Identify high-value and potential customers.
Generate actionable business insights for marketing strategies.
Tools Used
Power BI
CSV Dataset
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Dataset Information
The dataset contains customer details such as:
Customer ID
Gender
Age
Annual Income
Spending Score
Region
Purchase Frequency

Key Features
Customer Segmentation
Income and Spending Analysis
Customer Behavior Analysis
Interactive Dashboard
Targeted Business Insights

Customer Segments
The customers were categorized into:
High Income - High Spending
High Income - Low Spending
Low Income - High Spending
Low Income - Low Spending

Business Insights
Identified premium customers with high income and spending.
Identified potential customers for targeted promotions.
Analyzed customer purchasing behavior.
Supported data-driven marketing decisions.

Project Workflow
Data Collection

Dashboard Development
Insight Generation and Analysis

Expected Outcomes
Improved customer understanding.
Better customer targeting strategies.
Enhanced decision-making through analytics.
Identification of profitable customer groups.
Conclusion

The Customer Segmentation Dashboard successfully grouped customers based on income and spending behavior. The project provides valuable insights into customer preferences and supports effective marketing and customer relationship management strategies.
